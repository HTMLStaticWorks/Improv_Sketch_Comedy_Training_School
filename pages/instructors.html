Faculty Spotlight

Directing From The Heart of the Scene

Sarah spent eight seasons performing on Chicago’s iconic mainstages before relocating to New York to co-found The Setup. Her directing style strips away defensive irony and teaches actors how to care deeply about their stage partner’s needs.

"When an actor stops trying to win the argument and instead allows themselves to be profoundly affected by what their scene partner just said, the scene ignites into uncontrollable laughter. That is true ensemble connection."
