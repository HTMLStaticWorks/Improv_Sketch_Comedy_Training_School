Methodology

The Architecture of the Laugh

We reject "trying to be clever" or forcing punchlines. Our pedagogical model is built on three unbreakable pillars of truthful comedic performance.

Pillar One

Agreement Precedes Comedy

You cannot find the absurd until you establish the normal. By building instant reality with your scene partner, any small eccentric shift explodes with authentic comedic impact.

Pillar Two

Emotion Over Witty Jokes

Audiences connect with vulnerability. When a character genuinely cares about an absurd premise, the audience laughs with their soul rather than merely smirking at a pun.

Pillar Three

Rigorous Escalation

A funny idea must never stay static. We teach students how to identify the "Game", heightening stakes through repetition, justification, and thematic callbacks.
