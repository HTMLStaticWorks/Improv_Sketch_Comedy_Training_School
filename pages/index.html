The Setup Advantage

Why Train At The Setup

We blend Chicago-style long-form Harold discipline with modern sketch comedy writers' room mechanics.

Stage Time From Day 1

No waiting two years for a stage. Every class session takes place on our real black box theatre with professional audio & lighting.

Psychological Safety

Improv thrives when failure is celebrated. Our studios foster a supportive, joyful space where daring choices are encouraged.

Alumni Troupe Pipeline

Top ensemble performers are cast directly into our resident performance troupes with paid weekend mainstage slots.

Live Graduation Shows

Every 8-week level concludes with a public showcase where your friends and family see you perform under the lights.

Featured Training Tracks

Our sequential 4-tier conservatory takes you from comedic zero to mainstage ensemble player.

Level 1
8 Weeks • No Experience Required

Foundations of Improv

Unleash spontaneity, practice unconditional support with "Yes, And", and build fearless onstage presence through interactive games.

Explore Syllabus & Enroll
Level 2
8 Weeks • Level 1 Prerequisite

Two-Person Scene Work

Master emotional grounding, discover the "Game of the Scene", and develop memorable, hilarious character points of view.

Explore Syllabus & Enroll
Level 3
8 Weeks • Writers Room Format

Sketch Comedy Writing

Pitch premises, outline comedic escalation, draft parody beats, and polish a 4-sketch portfolio ready for TV submission.
