Est. 2018 • NYC Theater District

BUILT BY COMEDIANS, FOR COMEDIANS.

The Setup was born in the winter of 2018 when four veteran performers from Chicago's legendary Second City and LA's Groundlings pooled their savings to convert a shuttered vaudeville rehearsal space on 48th Street into a haven for unscripted theater.

Our mission was simple yet radical: replace toxic competition with joyful ensemble support, strip away theater bureaucracy, and give passionate students direct access to master directors who are actively shaping modern comedy.

412 Marquee Boulevard

Our historic 90-seat black box theatre has hosted over 1,200 public performances, 40 student graduation cohorts, and dozens of touring comedy stars.

Our Theatrical DNA

The Setup Core Code of Conduct

We believe comedy is at its most transcendent when everyone on stage feels completely safe to take enormous creative risks.

Value 01

Radical Ensemble Trust

The golden rule of improv: Make your scene partner look like a genius. When every person prioritizes their partner’s success over their own ego, brilliance happens naturally.

Value 02

Celebrate Every Failure

A flat joke isn’t a tragedy; it’s an invitation to explore something new. We treat missed beats as hilarious learning data that fuels your next breakthrough.

Value 03

Radically Inclusive Stage

Comedy flourishes when diverse life experiences converge. We actively champion scholarship programs, BIPOC ensembles, LGBTQ+ jams, and accessible classes.
