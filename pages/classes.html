Core Curriculum

The Four Conservatory Levels

Each level meets once weekly for 8 weeks (2.5 hours/session) and culminates in a ticketed student graduation showcase on our mainstage.

$295 / Term
Level 1 • 8 Weeks

Foundations of Improv

Format: In-person or Online
Break through anxiety and unlock spontaneous comedic reflexes. Discover the foundational principle of "Yes, And", spatial object work, and ensemble listening.

Enroll In Level 1
$325 / Term
Level 2 • 8 Weeks

Scene Work & Characters

Format: In-person Studio
Master 2-person grounded scenes. Develop high-status and low-status characters, uncover the comedic "unusual thing", and sustain organic emotional stakes.

Enroll In Level 2
$345 / Term
Level 3 • 8 Weeks

Advanced Harold & Formats

Format: In-person Mainstage
Tackle the legendary Chicago Harold format. Master openings, group games, second and third beat escalations, and seamless organic run-outs.

Enroll In Level 3
$395 / Term
Level 4 • 8 Weeks

Performance Troupe Prep

Format: Mainstage Residency
Prepare for professional auditions and festival runs. Craft an original 30-minute signature form with faculty mentorship and musical accompaniment.

Enroll In Level 4
